# Sprint domain

Own changes to Sprint drafts, publication, membership and graph eligibility.
The delivered working skill has level 1 and maps to narrow responsibility `sprints`.

Reuse the Sprint aggregate and public batch commands. Trace current membership,
graph snapshots and eligibility consumers together; graph validity alone does
not prove agreement of current projections. Follow the
[owning guarantees](../../../docs/workflows/sprints.md#владелец-и-основные-гарантии)
and [batch operations](../../../docs/workflows/sprints.md#пакетные-изменения).

Keep inter-task dependencies within one Sprint and independent Tasks standalone.
Preserve [result and ordering semantics](../../../docs/workflows/sprints.md#два-явных-вида-зависимостей);
operator scheduling agreements do not create executable dependencies.
Registration and graph formulation do not perform future Task stages. A ready
root may coexist with [newborn members](../../../docs/workflows/sprints.md#реальные-newborn-task-в-sprint-draft).
Use the public [return to draft](../../../docs/workflows/sprints.md#вернуть-опубликованный-sprint-в-черновик)
for permitted replanning, preserving completed members, accepted results, Task
goals and historical snapshots while updating current projections.

Check affected membership, publication and eligibility with independent expected
states and before/after effects. Keep exact replay, partial-publication recovery
and refusal semantics with their owners. Do not replace Task identities or edit
live storage to conceal inconsistent membership.
